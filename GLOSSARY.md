# Newsdesk

A personal news app that shows the owner's news ranked by how widely and how evenly it is covered.

## Language

**Story**:
One news event, as reported by one or more outlets.
_Avoid_: article, item, cluster (an article is one outlet's piece of a story)

**Outlet**:
A news organisation, counted once per story however many of its feeds carry it.
_Avoid_: source, feed, medio (a feed is one of an outlet's channels)

**Official source**:
An outlet that is the origin of the news itself (INDEC, BCRA, a company's own blog).

**Confirmed story**:
A story covered by 3 or more different outlets, or by an official source; in the Música and IA themes, 2 outlets are enough.
_Avoid_: multi-outlet story, verified

**Lean**:
An outlet's approximate place on the left/centre/right line, set by the owner.
_Avoid_: bias, ideology

**Unbiased**:
Covered by outlets from different leans, titled in neutral wording, with official numbers before opinions.
_Avoid_: neutral, objective

**Blind spot**:
An Argentine politics or economy story that outlets from one lean do not cover; shown as a mark on the story.

**Your artists**:
The artists the owner follows or plays most on Spotify, plus any they type in.
_Avoid_: favourites, bands

**Theme**:
A section the owner defines in their own words (e.g. "Música: Lanzamientos, Shows"); the AI keeps only stories that fit it.
_Avoid_: topic, category, tab
